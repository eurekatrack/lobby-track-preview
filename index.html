The ballot-measure fight

Uber and the trial lawyers each pushed initiatives aimed at the other. Uber's would cap attorney fees in car-accident cases; the trial lawyers' would widen rideshare companies' liability. Pain practices, which joined the fight against the fee cap, formed their own committee. None of these measures is on the November 2026 ballot.
